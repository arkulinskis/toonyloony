Three steps to your first cartoon

Most people make their first scene in about five minutes.

🧍 Build a character

Pick a body type (hero, robot, pup, birdy, caterpillar...), then mix heads, faces, hair, hats, clothes, colours and extras. Stuck? Roll the dice!

🕺 Teach it moves

Every character comes with ready-made moves like walk, run, sneak and dance. Make your own by bending the joints: each pose is a keyframe ◆.

🔴 Hold to record

Put characters on a stage, pick a move and a speed, then press and hold one and move it around. Rewind, and record the next one alongside.

Everything a cartoon needs

Characters from parts

12 body types, 18 heads, 15 kinds of eyes, 25 hats and hairdos, capes, umbrellas, jetpacks and 24 colours. Plus 37 props and 30 places to film in.

Joints and sticks

Drag the dots to bend elbows, knees, necks and tails. Onion skin shows your last pose, mirror flips it for walk cycles.

Hold-to-record studio

While you hold a character, the clock runs and everyone else replays their part, so scenes line up by themselves. Slow-mo makes fast scenes easy.

Voices with lip-sync

Record a line while your movie plays, turn it into a chipmunk, a giant or a robot, and watch the character's mouth move with it.

Sounds and music

22 cartoon sound effects (boing! ba-dum-tss!) and 8 music loops, made just for ToonyLoony. Or upload your own.

Wide, Reel or Square

Film in 16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, 1:1 for Instagram posts or 4:5 for the feed. Slide the camera to pick your shot.

Download and share

Save your cartoon as a video file, or (with an account) share a link so friends and family can watch it.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, all of it.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play as a guest and your toons are saved on your device. A free account keeps them safe and lets you share movies. Accounts are for people 16 or older, so younger makers can ask a parent or an older brother or sister.

Who can see my cartoons?

Only you, unless you share one. A shared movie plays for people who have its link. Sometimes we pick great ones to show in the Theater.

Can I make videos for Reels, TikTok or Instagram?

Yes. Pick Reel (9:16), Square (1:1) or Portrait (4:5) when you start a movie, or change the shape in the studio's Scene tab. The video you download comes out in that shape.

Does ToonyLoony listen to my microphone?

Only while you are recording a voice, after you press the red button. As a guest, your recordings never leave your device.

Does it work on a tablet or phone?

Yes: hold the character with your finger and move it. A bigger screen is comfier for the studio.

Can I use my own pictures and music?

Yes, if you made them or are allowed to use them. Please don't upload pictures of real people without asking them.
